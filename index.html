1994ENVIRONMENT

Environmental policy takes shape

Intel formalizes a company-wide commitment to environmental responsibility.

Intel’s environmental policy established a foundation for managing impacts across its operations. The company has continued to update its policies as its business and environmental priorities evolve.

2000sWATER

Water stewardship expands

Conservation, reuse, and watershed restoration become core parts of Intel’s water strategy.

Intel describes a three-part water approach: reduce water use in operations, reuse water through treatment, and restore water to local watersheds with community partners. The work supports its longer-term net-positive water ambition.

2012–20ENERGY

Energy efficiency accelerates

Efficiency projects save more than 4.5 billion kilowatt-hours of energy over the decade.

Intel reported saving over 4.5 billion kWh of energy between 2012 and 2020 through efficiency work. The company also expanded renewable electricity use during this period, including reaching 100% in the U.S. and EU and more than 70% globally by 2020.

20202030 GOALS

RISE goals set a new horizon

Intel announces 2030 goals for responsible, inclusive, and sustainable progress.

The RISE strategy set company-wide 2030 ambitions that include net-positive water, 100% renewable electricity across global operations, and zero total waste to landfill. Intel frames these goals as a long-term program requiring progress across operations and partnerships.

2022CLIMATE

Net-zero operations commitment

Intel pledges net-zero greenhouse gas emissions in global operations by 2040.

The operational commitment covers Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions. Intel’s plan includes investments in energy efficiency, renewable electricity, greenhouse gas abatement, process optimization, and chemical substitution.

2023RESTORATION

Water restoration gains ground

Intel reports restoring water to local watersheds through projects and operational savings.

In its 2023–24 report, Intel said it conserved about 10.2 billion gallons during 2023 and enabled projects that restored about 3.1 billion gallons to watersheds. Those actions advanced the company’s 2030 net-positive water goal.

2030RENEWABLES

A renewable energy target

Intel aims to use 100% renewable electricity across its global operations.

The 2030 target is part of Intel’s RISE goals. Reaching it involves a mix of renewable electricity procurement and on-site projects, supported by regional energy markets and supplier partnerships.

Building toward a lower-impact future

Long-range commitments extend climate action across operations and the value chain.

Looking back on the process

Project Reflections

What this project taught me about designing for people and the planet.

01

Creating a Webpage

Building this page helped me understand how a design can change while keeping the same content useful. I used Flexbox to keep the desktop timeline in one horizontal row, then used a media query to turn it into a vertical timeline on smaller screens. I learned that small details, like consistent spacing and image sizes, make a long page feel more organized. Adding hover and focus interactions also showed me how feedback can make a page feel easier to explore. Testing the layout at different widths helped me catch issues that were not obvious on a desktop screen.

02

Networking

Researching sustainability milestones showed me that large environmental goals depend on collaboration between many groups. Intel works with suppliers, utilities, governments, local communities, and restoration organizations to make progress on issues such as clean electricity and water. This helped me understand that professional networking is more than meeting people; it is also building relationships that let teams share knowledge and solve problems together. For this project, I used Intel reports and policy pages as sources to connect individual milestones to broader efforts. I learned to look for reliable information and recognize how partnerships can extend the impact of one company’s work.

03

Company Culture

Intel’s sustainability goals suggest that environmental responsibility is intended to be part of its work across operations, products, and partnerships. Exploring the timeline helped me see how company culture can shape priorities over many years, from managing environmental impacts to setting measurable goals. A culture that values improvement also depends on reporting progress and being clear about what remains a target. I learned that sustainability is connected to the choices employees make in manufacturing, engineering, and business planning. This project encouraged me to consider how shared values can influence the way a technology company works and grows.
